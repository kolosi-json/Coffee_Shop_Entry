# Coffee_Shop_Entry
My client tasked me to build and manage a small database for her coffee shop. She noted me to keep track on her regulars, the menu, and the flow of transaction.

My client provided me full details about her newly opened cafe, and she required my aid to discover what possible strategies should we apply for her business to grow. As I ask important questions regarding about her goal and vision for her business, I noted that as for the moment, her coffee shop does not need to grow right now, I explained that a business growing without any solid foundations regarding with managing inventories, partners, and the site will result into confusing mess; challenges like growing numbers of customers while manpower is short can potentially result to customer complaints about slow service, ordering ingredients while the stock warehouse is small can cause production of beverages to freeze due to ingredients easily running out and it will take a while for delivery to arrive, partners who are new to the workflow will take time to get used to the setup, and more . I recommended to her that as of right now, our goal is to maintain consistent and healthy profit growth, so when the time comes, she can utilize her earnings to improve her workplace at a steady rate, and as the coffee shop grows, we can afford to manage possible risks and challenges with less difficulty.

As for our first project, we focus more on getting to know our customers and what items they usually purchase, and to prioritize what ingredients should she always have on stock. Potentially, we can use the most favourite item as opportunity to market it and to recommend it for newer customers.
